Manufacturing Non-Disclosure, Intellectual Property & Non-Circumvention Agreement

ELECTRONIC ACCEPTANCE & INCORPORATION BY REFERENCE

This Agreement is digitally hosted at: https://primexaglobal.com/nda.php

By executing any associated contract, certificate, or purchase order that references this URL, the signing entity automatically accepts and agrees to be legally bound by all provisions within this Agreement as the "Receiving Party," without the necessity of a physical signature on this specific document.

The purpose of this Agreement is to define the binding provisions regarding confidentiality, the safeguarding of Intellectual Property Rights, specific production limitations, and non-circumvention requirements governing all external entities providing manufacturing or engineering services to Primexa Global.

1. IDENTIFICATION OF THE PARTIES

This Manufacturing Non-Disclosure, Intellectual Property & Non-Circumvention Agreement (the "Agreement") is made effective upon the execution of an associated contract or certificate by and between:

Primexa Global (ANSDK trading PVT Ltd), a business organization duly organized and existing pursuant to the statutes of India, with its principal registered office located at M.NO. 234/2 P.NO. 20, SRIRAM NAGAR, PHULAMBRI, Aurangabad - 431111, Maharashtra (hereinafter termed the "Disclosing Party", which terms shall include its successors-in-interest, legal assigns, affiliates, subsidiaries, directors, and authorized personnel);

AND
The entity, organization, or individual that signs an associated contract, purchase order, or certificate referencing this Agreement (hereinafter termed the "Receiving Party" or "Supplier"). The Disclosing Party and the Receiving Party shall be referred to individually as a "Party" and together as the "Parties."

2. OBJECTIVES AND DEFINITIONS

The Parties contemplate entering into mutual deliberations and commercial activities concerning the assessment, estimation, design, technical fabrication, machining, additive manufacturing, validation, and delivery of specialized components and engineering Deliverables as commissioned by the Disclosing Party.

In the course of these professional engagements, proprietary data of a technical, financial, or strategic nature will be provided by the Disclosing Party. The Parties formally recognize the inherent value and sensitive status of such Confidential Information and hereby pledge to safeguard it pursuant to the binding stipulations of this Agreement.

2.1 Agreement: refers to this Manufacturing Non-Disclosure, Intellectual Property & Non-Circumvention Agreement.
2.2 Confidential Information: the comprehensive body of proprietary technical and commercial data further detailed in Section 3, provided by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party.
2.3 Intellectual Property Rights: all legal protections for inventions, CAD models, software code, trade secrets, manufacturing methodologies, and derivative technical works belonging to the Disclosing Party, whether registered or unregistered.
2.4 Project: any specific engineering task, quotation request, production assignment, or collaborative technical activity initiated between the Parties.
2.5 Customer: any government agency, enterprise, or private client introduced by the Disclosing Party in relation to a specific Project.
2.6 Deliverables: the tangible or digital results of a Project, including machined components, assembly prototypes, inspection documentation, and associated technical reports.
2.7 Applicable Laws: all relevant Indian statutes and international regulations governing export control, intellectual property protection, and information security.
2.8 Work Product: any innovation, technical data, or material generated by the Receiving Party that utilizes the Disclosing Party's proprietary information or assets during the performance of a Project.

3. PROPRIETARY AND CONFIDENTIAL DATA

3.1 Classification of Confidential Information: Within the scope of this Agreement, the term "Confidential Information" signifies any data shared by Primexa Global (ANSDK trading PVT Ltd), its corporate associates, entities, patrons, or technical advisors. Such disclosure may occur through any medium—including digital, verbal, visual, or physical—and includes all materials explicitly marked as proprietary or that would logically be considered sensitive given the professional context.

Confidential Information encompasses, but is not restricted to:
(a) Technical Data: All engineering schematics, CAD architectures (including native, STEP, STL, and Parasolid formats), 2D/3D visualizations, GD&T parameters, manufacturing records, tooling specifications, assembly workflows, CNC and CAM instruction sets, additive manufacturing profiles, metallurgical procedures, simulation analytics, algorithmic code, and any experimental prototypes.
(b) Commercial Intelligence: Strategic business frameworks, pricing structures, cost analyses, procurement logs, financial projections, market expansion strategies, operational timelines, and all sensitive data regarding customer or supplier relationships.
(c) Intellectual Assets: Proprietary know-how, trade secrets, novel inventions, patentable works, industrial aesthetics, copyrighted materials, specialized production methodologies, and corporate roadmaps belonging exclusively to the Disclosing Party.
(d) Tangible Assets: Physical prototypes, machined hardware, specialized assemblies, casting dies, tooling equipment, testing coupons, and any other physical items or embedded media provided by the Disclosing Party.

3.2 Exclusions from Protection: The confidentiality obligations herein shall not apply to information that the Receiving Party can validate via objective written evidence as: information already in possession through lawful means without restriction; information entering the public domain without violation; information acquired from an unrestricted third party; or information developed autonomously without reference to proprietary assets.
3.3 Legally Mandated Disclosure: Should the Receiving Party be compelled by judicial order or statute to reveal Confidential Information, it shall provide immediate written notification prior to disclosure.

4. DUTIES AND OBLIGATIONS OF THE RECEIVING PARTY

4.1 Maintenance of Secrecy: The Receiving Party is strictly mandated to preserve absolute confidentiality.
4.2 Authorized Scope of Use: Utilization of Confidential Information is restricted exclusively to the professional assessment, design, technical fabrication, validation, and delivery of specialized components commissioned by the Disclosing Party.
4.3 Regulated Access and Disclosure: Proprietary data may be shared only with personnel who possess a legitimate professional requirement and are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.
4.4 Prohibition of Market Exploitation: Strictly prohibited from manufacturing, commercializing, or exploiting any components or derivative technologies predicated upon Confidential Information without express written consent.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS & ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

5.1 Ownership of Intellectual Property: All Intellectual Property Rights and Work Product generated or disclosed throughout the Project remain the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party.
5.2 Safeguarding Intellectual Assets: Strictly prohibited from duplication, modification, reverse engineering, or commercial exploitation without prior written authorization.
5.3 Restrictions on Artificial Intelligence (AI): Refrain from uploading, processing, or utilizing any engineering data, CAD models, or Confidential Information within any third-party Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning, or Generative AI frameworks without express written consent.

6. MANUFACTURING LIMITATIONS

Absent prior written authorization, the Receiving Party shall not exceed authorized volumes, fabricate similar/identical components for external entities, retain tooling following services, engage in digital scanning/reverse engineering, or subcontract any segment of production.

7. DISPOSAL OF PROPRIETARY DATA & 8. DURATION

Upon request or termination, the Receiving Party shall immediately return or permanently destroy all Confidential Information. This Agreement remains operative for a term of five (5) years, with confidentiality obligations surviving for a subsequent period of five (5) years.

9. JURISDICTION & 10. NON-CIRCUMVENTION OBLIGATIONS

Governed by the statutes of India with exclusive jurisdiction residing in the courts of Maharashtra, India. Throughout the term and for two (2) years thereafter, the Receiving Party pledges not to solicit, contract with, or circumvent the Disclosing Party by dealing directly with any introduced Customer.

11. LEGAL REMEDIES AND INDEMNIFICATION

The Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief for irreparable harm. The Receiving Party pledges to defend and indemnify the Disclosing Party against all claims, damages, legal fees, and liabilities arising from any breach of this Agreement.
